# Afterline systemic text prototype

This branch contains a 12-week playable response-agency campaign designed to demonstrate replayability through simulation rather than branching story content.

## Five systemic layers

1. **Generated entities** — campaign seed generates operatives, districts, incidents, rivals, stats, and outcomes.
2. **Persistent simulation** — unresolved incidents gain pressure, districts lose stability, and future cases become harder.
3. **Meaningful scarcity** — the agency receives six command points and six total operative focus each week.
4. **Competing priorities** — acting on one incident consumes money, command, and operative focus while other incidents continue moving.
5. **Long-term measurement** — league rank, agency score, public trust, funds, solved/failed records, operative XP, and district stability persist across the 12-week campaign.

The operations feed describes results produced by the simulation. It is not the primary game system.

## Controls

- Select one of the generated operatives.
- Apply Intel, Field, or Influence actions to any active incident.
- Match operative strengths and action types to the incident for stronger progress.
- End the week to advance unresolved incidents, rival agencies, district stability, and case generation.
- Complete the 12-week campaign or lose the mandate if public trust collapses.

Campaign state is saved locally in the browser. Use **Generate a different campaign** after a season to create a new seeded world.
